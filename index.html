Information We Collect

BFinance may collect and process the following information when necessary to provide app functionality:

Your email address may be used to:

Your financial data is primarily stored locally on your device. BFinance does not sell your financial information or use it for advertising purposes.

Account-related information, such as your email address and information required for authentication or password recovery, may be processed by our backend services when necessary to provide these features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal or financial information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to provide authentication, account recovery, or other essential app services.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
